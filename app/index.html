    Sources & method

    Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles (the two agreeing on all 55). Every guide is written for this entry in the author's own words and names its sources beside it — the official description for what a trophy asks for, and the GTA Wiki (gta.fandom.com) where a method relies on how Grand Theft Auto games work (mission replay, the wanted system, 100% completion). No guide text is copied from another guide. This app fetches nothing: it loads only its own files and works with the network off. No Rockstar Games logo or official artwork is used; the look is our own.

    Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and never leaves your machine unless you export it. Missable flags mean: do the named thing during the mission or attempt, or replay it — Grand Theft Auto games let you replay story missions. There are no online trophies in the official list, so the Online filter is empty by design.
